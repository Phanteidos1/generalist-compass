The 8 generalist types

Everyone gets one main type and a second one. None is better than another; each fits a different kind of work.

The Opener

Starts things from zero: new markets, launches, first deals. Bored once it runs.

Thrives in: young companies launching something new
The Connector

Puts people and opportunities together, and deals happen. Reads people fast.

Thrives in: partnerships, business development, sales
The Translator

Bridges worlds: cultures, industries, languages, teams. Explains the complex simply.

Thrives in: international teams, product and customer roles
The Fixer

Walks into chaos and makes it work. Calm when things break.

Thrives in: turnarounds, operations, crisis moments
The Operator

Turns ideas into systems that run without them. Spots the bottleneck.

Thrives in: scaling companies, operations, chief of staff
The Explorer

Learns fast across fields and spots angles others miss. Tests before committing.

Thrives in: research, strategy, new ventures
The Storyteller

Shapes how people see something, and they follow. Builds a brand or a room.

Thrives in: marketing, community, content, events
The Strategist

Sees the pattern and decides where to go. A calm risk-taker.

Thrives in: founder's office, strategy, investing
Who made this

Why this exists

I'm Mike. I've sold diamonds in Antwerp, built a buying operation in Latin America from nothing, filled rooms in Brussels, and played poker for a living. On paper, that's four different people. Every job board agreed.

But it was never four careers. It was one move, made in four places: walk into something unfinished and make it real. It took me years to see that thread, and I needed someone, or something, to say it back to me.

So I built the tool I wish I'd had. If your path looks scattered from the outside, this is for you.

How to get your LinkedIn contacts file
  1. On LinkedIn, open Me, then Settings & Privacy, then Data privacy.
  2. Click Get a copy of your data, tick only Connections, and request it.
  3. You get an email, usually within minutes. Download and unzip it.
  4. Pick the file Connections.csv below.

The file is read in your browser. Only names, roles and companies of your most relevant contacts are sent to the AI. Nothing is stored.
